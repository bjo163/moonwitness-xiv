# Evidence K4-002 — polarity, speaker, and quotation annotations

## Status

- Status: complete locally; exact-SHA hosted CI and protected promotion remain delivery gates.
- Date: 2026-10-16 (Asia/Jakarta).
- Dependencies: K4-001 text offsets and K3-001 scoped assertion contract.
- Scope: a versioned annotation contract and pure validation/projection helpers in `@moonwitness/research`.

## Result

- Added `ontology/domains/research-semantic-annotation.schema.json` and strict TypeScript types for polarity (`positive`, `negative`, `ambiguous`, `unknown`), evidence-linked cue spans, quotation mode, speaker identity, and review state.
- Cue and source spans carry UTF-8 byte and Unicode-scalar offsets. Their basis is explicitly the resolved evidence excerpt after its selector range has been applied. Validation checks the excerpt-relative offsets, exact cue text, assertion/evidence identity, source hash, passage identity, selector range, quote, and evidence direction.
- Explicit negation can only annotate negative polarity and explicit affirmation can only annotate positive polarity. Ambiguous/unknown annotations remain review-required. Quoted/reported speech requires both a speaker and an exact source cue.
- Accepted or rejected annotation decisions require reviewer identity and time. Projection preserves polarity and speaker context; it does not rewrite or replace the assertion, source text, or review event history.
- Synthetic fixture tests cover a negative cue, a positive quoted statement that remains speaker-scoped, missing attribution, inconsistent cues, stale text, wrong evidence binding, and a nonzero selector range preceded by a multibyte character. The test wording is synthetic and is not claimed as source content or added to the corpus.

## Boundaries

- This is a deterministic contract and projection layer, not a language analyzer, authenticated review API, durable annotation store, or authorization system.
- Callers must resolve the evidence through the approved local corpus and authorize the reviewer through the K3-005 workflow before recording trusted decisions. A schema-valid reviewer field alone is not proof of authorization.
- No polarity, speaker, or quotation interpretation is inferred from timestamps or assigned to any real source passage by this milestone.

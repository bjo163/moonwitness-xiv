## Scope check on the accepted-decision gate — 2026-10-25

This unit changed no code. It re-reads the gate the card's acceptance line depends on, because that line — "no equation of narrative persona with historical/theological person without review" — is only as strong as what the validator can actually tell about a decision.

- What the gate does check (`packages/research/src/persona-canon.ts:89-135`): an association needs exactly one decision plus its candidate; the decision must itself pass `validateIdentityDecision`; its `outcome` must be `'accepted'`; `decision.id` must equal the association's `decision_id`; `decision.candidate_id` must equal the candidate's id; both the persona and the associated identity must be one of the candidate's two endpoints and not each other; and the decision's preserved conflict set must equal the candidate's conflict set exactly.
- What it cannot check: `acceptedDecision` and `candidate` are parameters the caller supplies (`persona-canon.ts:46-50`), and `recordIdentityDecision` (`packages/research/src/identity-reconciliation.ts:128-161`) is a pure constructor that copies `outcome`, `reviewer_id`, `reviewed_at` and `evidence_refs` straight out of its `input` argument (`:148-157`). It never compares `reviewer_id` to an authenticated actor, never consults a workflow instance, and never requires the reviewer to differ from the persona's author. So a caller can obtain a structurally perfect "accepted" decision for any candidate by asking for one, and `persona-canon.ts:114` will read the conflation authorization the acceptance line reserves for a review.
- Consequence for review. This is the self-certification path K3-005's acceptance forbids for research review events, still open on the identity-decision surface: K3-005 governs `ResearchReviewEvent` through a locked workflow transaction, but the persona validator consumes a standalone decision object, not that workflow. The card cannot be accepted as review-authorizing until an accepted identity decision is produced only by an authenticated, non-self-certifying reviewer path.
- Why the fix is not this card's scope. `K5-005` declares `corpus/reference/` and `ontology/mappings/`. The two candidate fixes live elsewhere: (a) a brand on reviewer-accepted decisions — the equivalent of what K4-003 landed for evidence on 2026-10-25, where `resolveResearchEvidenceReference` registers each minted object in a module-private `WeakSet` and `isAttestedEvidenceResolution` refuses anything a caller hand-built — belongs to the K3-003 contract in `packages/research/src/identity-reconciliation.ts`, which `relation.ts:323` also consumes, so it is a cross-card contract change; (b) resolving the decision from trusted, company-scoped storage belongs to `@moonwitness/orm-knowledge` and the review API, and is blocked by the same unresolved owner decision recorded under K3-007 on 2026-10-25: a graph release is keyed by `corpus_key` alone and its records carry only `key/kind/visibility/content`, so there is no company column to scope a persona's review history against without either populating one at write time or issuing per-company releases.
- Owner decision needed, in this order: (1) confirm that a `mw:persona:*` → `mw:entity|agent:*` association may be authorized only by an authenticated reviewer through the K3-005 workflow, which makes the brand/attestation change a K3-003 work item; (2) settle the K3-007 company-scoping question, because until it is settled a company-scoped review-history read for personas has no storage shape to target; (3) name the source scope for real alias/role mappings — `source_refs` currently checks internal attribution only (`K5-005` Status above), and the only approved sources remain Tanzil Q7:46 and WEBP Genesis 1:1, which carry no persona corpus.
- What is already correct and needs no owner action: namespace separation (`mw:persona:*` never collapses into `mw:entity|agent:*` by default), alias/role source attribution against `source_refs`, rejection of `same_as` and canonicalization properties, and the synthetic-only fixture posture. No record was written and no association was authorized by this check.

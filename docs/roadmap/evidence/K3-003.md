# Evidence K3-003 — human-reviewed identity reconciliation

## Status

- Status: complete locally; hosted CI and protected promotion remain delivery gates.
- Date: 2026-10-16 (Asia/Jakarta).
- Dependencies: K3-001 scoped assertion contract and K1-002 canonical identity/alias rules.
- Scope: typed match candidates and explicit identity decisions. Candidate creation never creates an identity equivalence relation.

## Result

- Added a versioned identity-decision schema and research API for candidate validation, human decision recording, and decision summaries.
- Candidates remain unresolved hypotheses. Accepted/rejected decisions require evidence references, a reviewer ID, a review timestamp, and preservation of every candidate conflict.
- Alias observations must refer to one of the candidate identities; homonym, multilingual alias, competing identity, and other conflict kinds remain explicit.
- Duplicate conflict IDs and same-identity candidates are rejected. Decisions from different candidates cannot be summarized together, and invalid decisions cannot enter summaries.
- Contradictory accepted/rejected decisions remain in the result as `conflicting_decisions`; no decision history is overwritten.

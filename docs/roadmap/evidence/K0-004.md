## Result

- Missing or unclear rights default to `unknown`; unknown, restricted, disputed, review-pending, or withdrawn rights fail closed for byte redistribution and publication.
- Metadata-only is the fallback and excludes source passage text, OCR, translations, and excerpts.
- The policy requires exact edition/scope decisions, integrity evidence, attribution, provenance, and an assigned complaint/takedown process before publication.
- The maintainer approved this general policy and accepted the rights-steward responsibility. This is not legal advice or clearance for any particular source.

## Correctness invariants

- Absence of primary text is **NEEDS_SOURCE**, not `COMPLETE`.
- Editorial V10 description is **not** a human-approved final canon.
- A historical repo entry, story synopsis or issue closure is not proof of app deployment, poster rights, a fulfilled prayer or a real-world outcome.
- Never compare or automatically merge persons, spiritual figures, character aliases or divine names because their labels resemble one another.
- Distinct models: four Soul pillars vs four Empire pillars; 99 Asma vs 99 G.O.D. fictional roles vs 99 Human States; Wings 10 vs Aetherion 10 realms.
- Preserve an original source and correction as separate immutable versions; do not overwrite art on text revision.
- Public-facing publications require their own explicit source/privacy/rights and reviewer decision.

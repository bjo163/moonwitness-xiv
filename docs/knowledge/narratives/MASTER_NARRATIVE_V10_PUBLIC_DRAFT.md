## Purpose and publication boundary

This document is the public-safe, **chapter-by-chapter editorial companion** to the privately held 99-chapter V10 manuscript assembled in conversation. It is deliberately **not** a verbatim transcription of that private manuscript; no private chat export, diary extract, unique personal timestamp, media binary, or copyright-restricted lyric is placed in this repository. Preserve the full user-authored and conversational originals privately with original bytes and explicit authorization before ingestion. Do not silently label AI synthesis as author-original.

The chapter count `99` is an editorial organizing choice, not a claim to divine authority, miraculous accuracy or measurable “99× research depth.” Any line that describes a religious experience is **reported or interpreted**, not externally certified communication or approval. Narrative persona names are fictional/poetic unless source-specific evidence establishes something else. Open issues and source conflicts are **part of** the archive, not defects to hide.
